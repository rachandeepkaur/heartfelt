Design a beautiful Valentine-themed landing page for an AI-powered greeting card app that creates personalized animated video cards.

The mood should feel romantic, warm, playful, and emotionally expressive — not cheesy, not corporate. The design should feel premium, modern, and delightful.

Overall aesthetic:
- Soft romantic gradients (pink, rose, lavender, peach, cream)
- Subtle glowing highlights
- Floating heart particles in background
- Gentle parallax motion
- Soft blur and glassmorphism panels
- Smooth rounded edges
- Elegant spacing and breathable layout

Background animation:
- Slow floating hearts and sparkles
- Soft gradient waves moving subtly
- Animated light glow behind hero section
- Motion should be calm and dreamy, not distracting

Typography:
- Elegant serif headline font (romantic feel)
- Clean modern sans-serif for UI text
- Handwritten accent font for emotional highlights
- Large expressive hero typography

Hero section:
- Headline: emotional and bold
- Subheadline describing AI personalization
- Animated preview of greeting card video
- Primary CTA button: glowing, hover pulse effect
- Secondary CTA: soft outline button
- Floating animated card mockups

Interactive buttons:
- Smooth hover glow
- Heart pulse animation on click
- Micro-bounce interaction
- Gradient shimmer effect
- Rounded pill shapes

Workflow explanation section:
Use animated illustrations to show steps:

Step 1: Choose occasion
Step 2: Tell us about your loved one
Step 3: AI creates personalized art + message
Step 4: Send animated video card

Each step should have:
- Animated icon or mini animation
- Floating transitions
- Scroll-triggered motion
- Soft fade and slide effects

Card showcase section:
- Carousel of animated card previews
- Hover to play animation
- Soft drop shadows
- Glowing frame on focus

Emotional highlights section:
- Testimonials floating in soft bubbles
- Handwritten quotes
- Subtle sparkle animation

Footer:
- Calm gradient fade
- Minimal icons
- Romantic tone

Mood keywords:
dreamy, heartfelt, magical, intimate, joyful, modern romance

Avoid:
- harsh neon colors
- heavy dark themes
- cluttered UI
- corporate dashboard style
- overly childish visuals

The page should feel like a mix of:
modern design + romantic storytelling + creative studio energy.
